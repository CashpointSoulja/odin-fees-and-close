Floors and caps exist - the amounts were not found in public sources

Odin's help centre states: "Deal fees are capped at both ends, with a floor price and a ceiling price." The actual floor and ceiling amounts were not found in Odin's public website or help centre as of 30 Oct 2026, so no exact total can be worked out from the figures found there.

Follow-on deals have a reduced minimum fee

Odin's glossary says it "offers reduced minimum deal fees for follow-on deals", and the reduced amount was not found in public sources as of 30 Oct 2026. It only binds when the minimum applies, so ask on deal two onwards.

One party pays the fee - you choose which

Odin's fee can be paid by the syndicate lead, the investee company, or investors pro-rata (a £50k investor pays more of it than a £5k one). It cannot be split between parties. Carry is entered as a fixed percentage in deal setup - Odin says no in-product mechanism exists for hurdles or other performance terms.

Five things to ask Odin before you close

The published numbers get you to a shortlist of questions, not to a total. These are the gaps this page could not fill from public sources.

  1. 1Which pricing tier applies to my deal - and what are its fee floor and cap? (Odin says floors and caps exist; the amounts were not found in public sources, and existing customers may be on legacy terms.)
  2. 2What is the reduced minimum fee for a follow-on deal?
  3. 3Who should pay the fee on this deal - me, the company, or my investors pro-rata - and what does each choice do to my carry maths?
  4. 4What does a rolling close cost on my tier - the always-on SPV from $12,000/year, or an edge-case extra fee?
  5. 5Which services trigger the "additional fees may apply" line on the SPV price card?
